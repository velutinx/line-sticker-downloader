LINE Sticker Downloader

Paste a pack URL from the LINE store and get every sticker in a single zip — PNG files, plus GIFs for animated packs.

Find a pack at store.line.me and copy its URL.

How long to wait after clicking each sticker to detect animated versions. Raise it if GIFs are missing.

Heads-up: for animated packs, both the original PNGs and the converted GIFs are included in the zip. After extracting, keep whichever set you need and delete the other.

Downloads are generated server-side with a headless browser. First request after a period of inactivity may take 30–60 s to warm up.
